Heritage & Judicial Ethos

Five Decades of Respected Standing at the Indian Bar

Poddar & Associates (P&A) was established in 1969 by Senior Advocate Mr. Binod Poddar, who previously served as the Advocate General and Additional Advocate General for the State of Jharkhand. Commencing practice in 1967 and 1969 respectively, the founding partners built an institution anchored in rigorous jurisprudence and courtroom dignity.

As a full-service law firm, we provide pragmatic, authoritative solutions to public sector enterprises, industrial corporations, commercial banks, and individuals. Our chambers combine veteran trial litigators with domain-specialist legal analysts.
